Privacy Policy — QVIEW Companion

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how QVIEW Companion (package tv.qview.companion, "the App", "we", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our Android mobile companion application for QVIEW WatchParty.

1. Information We Collect and Process

  • Anonymous Account & Profile Information: When you use the App, an anonymous authentication identifier (auth.uid) is created via Supabase Auth. You may optionally enter a display nickname and select preferred room categories/interests, which are synced to our backend so other participants can see your nickname in rooms and chat. QVIEW Companion does not require an email address, password, or Google account sign-in.
  • Device Identifiers (Anti-Abuse & Security): A hardware-derived Widevine DRM device identifier (or a locally generated random UUID fallback if unavailable) is stored locally and sent to our backend strictly for anti-spam protection, room rating cooldown enforcement, and enforcing room or platform bans. It is never used for advertising or cross-app tracking.
  • Camera Access (On-Device QR Scanning Only): If you grant the optional Camera permission (android.permission.CAMERA), the App uses your camera solely to scan QVIEW WatchParty QR codes (for joining rooms or pairing with Android TV). Camera frames are processed locally on your device using Android CameraX and Google ML Kit Barcode Scanning and are never recorded, stored, or transmitted to our servers or third parties.
  • User-Generated Content & In-App Messages: Watch party room metadata created or edited by you (room titles, descriptions, schedules, categories, privacy settings, PINs, stream and poster URLs), room join requests, room ratings, and real-time chat messages sent during watch parties.
  • Local App Preferences: Your nickname, interests, last entered room PIN, recent stream/poster URLs, approved room invites, and appearance settings are stored locally on your device via Android DataStore and SharedPreferences.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • App Functionality: Joining and managing watch party rooms, pairing with Android TV devices, sending and displaying live chat messages, handling room join requests, and scanning QR codes.
  • Safety, Moderation & Anti-Abuse: Preventing spam, enforcing host room bans and platform blocks, and limiting duplicate room ratings.

3. No Ads, No Payments, No Data Selling

QVIEW Companion does not display third-party advertisements, does not include ad-tracking SDKs, does not process in-app purchases or payment details, and never sells or rents user data to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Infrastructure

All network traffic is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/WSS). We rely on the following service providers strictly to operate the App:

  • Supabase: Anonymous authentication, database storage, and server-side RPC operations.
  • Cloudflare Workers: Real-time WebSocket room synchronization and chat message delivery.
  • Google ML Kit Barcode Scanning: On-device QR code recognition.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You can clear locally stored data at any time by clearing App storage in Android system settings or uninstalling the App. You can also request deletion of your server-side profile and associated user data at any time via our Account & Data Deletion Request Form or by emailing us.

Deletion requests are processed within 7 business days. Minimal security and abuse-prevention records (such as active ban hashes) may be retained as necessary to protect users and enforce service rules.

6. Children's Privacy

QVIEW Companion is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required by applicable local law, including 18 where applicable), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
