Privacy Policy — QVIEW WatchParty

Effective Date: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how QVIEW WatchParty ("the App", "we", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our Android TV and companion applications.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, and user account identifiers provided when you voluntarily sign in with Google (OAuth), as well as an authentication UID used to connect to watch party rooms.
  • Device or Other Identifiers: A randomly generated app-scoped installation identifier (Device ID) used to link saved rooms and device preferences to your account.
  • Purchase History: Google Play Billing purchase tokens, product IDs, and subscription/donation status used to verify and restore QVIEW PRO access and voluntary supporter purchases. Payment card and billing details are processed exclusively by Google Play and are never collected or stored by us.
  • User-Generated Content & In-App Messages: Watch party rooms created or saved by users (room PINs, titles, schedules, stream links) and room chat messages displayed during watch parties.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • App Functionality: Creating and joining rooms, synchronizing playback, displaying room chat, restoring saved rooms and PRO status.
  • Account Management: Signing in with Google and managing your user profile.
  • Security & Fraud Prevention: Verifying Google Play purchase tokens and enforcing room limits.

3. Data Storage and Infrastructure

All collected data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/WSS). We do not sell user data and do not share data with third-party advertisers or ad networks. We rely on the following service providers:

  • Supabase (authentication and database storage)
  • Cloudflare Workers (real-time room synchronization and chat delivery)
  • Google Play Services (Google Sign-In and Google Play Billing)

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time via our Account Deletion Request Form.

Requests are processed within 7 business days. Anonymized Google Play Billing transaction records (without personal identifiers or email) may be retained for up to 90 days (or as required by applicable law) for accounting, fraud prevention, and dispute resolution.

5. Children's Privacy

QVIEW WatchParty is not directed to children under 13 (or 18 where applicable), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
